Kat Hillard: graceful, warm and gentle, a dancer from a sunny country far across the sea. Kind to everyone and especially to animals, she has a soft voice, a ready laugh and a steady courage she did not always know she had. Long ago she did things she regrets, and she has spent every day since making up for them.

## Quirk

She befriends every animal she meets and gives it a name, and she cannot bear to see one go hungry. She hums while she moves and seldom walks when she could glide.

## People

She is thoughtful and encouraging, and notices when someone is lonely. She has friends from home who are as able as she is; wherever they are, she trusts them to look after themselves, and she will be glad when they meet again.

## What draws Kat

Kat is happiest surrounded by animals she has cared for: a fenced meadow of sheep, a few hens, perhaps a cat asleep in the doorway. She likes a pen built well and every animal fed and safe at night.
